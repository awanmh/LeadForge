Privacy & Data Ownership Policy

1. Information We Collect

When prospective clients submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect only the provided contact information: name, business email address, optional service interest, and message content. We do not track visitors across third-party websites or deploy tracking beacons.

2. Technical Isolation & Storage

Inquiries are processed by an isolated API service and persisted in a private PostgreSQL database (leadforge_app). Inbound submissions are logically separated from our public editorial CMS (leadforge_cms). There are no public API endpoints or client-accessible routes that expose captured inquiries.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Prospect inquiry records are retained for a maximum duration of 180 days to facilitate project scoping and contractual evaluation. After 180 days, records are scheduled for automated purge or manual review.

You may request immediate deletion of your inquiry details at any time by contacting our operators at inquiries@forma.studio.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose prospect inquiry records to external brokers, advertising platforms, or marketing networks under any circumstances.

Last updated: October 2026. LeadForge architectural privacy baseline.
